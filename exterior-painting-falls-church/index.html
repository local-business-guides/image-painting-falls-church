How to prepare and what to ask

A good conversation about the project can help you avoid missed details. Ask which surfaces are included, what preparation is planned, and whether related repairs should be considered before painting. If you want a deck stained as well as painted areas refreshed, mention both needs when requesting the estimate.

Use this short checklist before the estimate:

  • Identify each exterior area you want reviewed.
  • Point out damage, peeling, or previous repairs you have noticed.
  • Separate painting needs from deck staining or carpentry questions.
  • Ask how pressure washing fits into the proposed work.
  • Confirm the written scope before the project is scheduled.

One mistake is treating pressure washing as a substitute for discussing surface preparation and repairs. Another is leaving a deck or trim concern out of the request, then expecting it to be part of the original scope. Bring up each area early so you and the company can talk through what is needed.
