Plan the project before choosing a painter

House painters Washington DC homeowners contact should be able to discuss the areas to be painted, the condition of the surfaces, and the work that may be needed before paint goes on. Start with a simple list of rooms or exterior areas. Note peeling or damaged spots, walls that need patching, and any surfaces you want left untouched. That gives you a clearer way to compare what each proposed project includes.

It also helps to decide what matters most: changing the color, refreshing worn paint, or addressing surface damage. If you’re unsure about colors, ask whether color consulting is available. A useful estimate should be written and detailed enough for you to understand the planned work.

What to discuss and prepare

The exact work depends on the surfaces and your goals. A painting project may involve cleaning, drywall repairs, carpentry, or other preparation, as well as painting the selected areas. For an exterior request, describe which parts of the home need attention. For an interior project, name the rooms and mention any trim, cabinets, or damaged wall areas you want considered.

Before an estimate, you can make a short checklist:

  • Identify each room or exterior surface you want included.
  • Point out visible damage, peeling paint, or areas needing repair.
  • Share colors you’re considering, or ask about color consulting.
  • Ask what preparation, painting, and cleanup are included.
  • Confirm the address and timing before planning around the work.

Move small belongings away from the work area when practical, and ask the estimator what else should be cleared. Avoid choosing a painter based only on a broad description or verbal scope. Confirm that the written estimate reflects the areas and repairs you discussed.
